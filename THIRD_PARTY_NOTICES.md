# Third-party software

Chatlog Plus is an independent companion service. Its releases may include a Windows Chatlog
core binary built from the pinned source commit below plus the patch in
`patches/chatlog-safe-local.patch`. The corresponding source and modification are available in
this repository. No release includes WeChat databases, decryption keys, media files, speech
models, transcripts, or account data.

- [Chatlog](https://github.com/imldy/chatlog), pinned at commit
  `eb9572ee028cbaa72a50749b9dfe6630b317228f`, is distributed under the Apache-2.0 license.
- [faster-whisper](https://github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper) is distributed under the MIT license.
